How long does local SEO take for trades in the UK?

Updated 8 September 2026 8 min read

Most UK trades businesses see the first movement in local search within 4 to 8 weeks, meaningful lead volume between 3 and 6 months, and a settled position in competitive towns between 6 and 12 months. Google Business Profile work moves fastest because the map pack responds to profile quality and reviews. Organic rankings for competitive service terms take longest, because they depend on content and links that accumulate.

The realistic timeline, month by month

These are typical patterns for a trades business in a UK town, assuming steady work rather than a one-off burst of effort. A business starting with no Google Business Profile moves faster at the beginning than one that has been optimised for years.

Why local SEO is not instant

Three things create the delay, and none of them is an agency dragging its feet.

  • Google has to recrawl and reassess. Changes to your profile and pages are not evaluated the moment you make them.
  • Trust signals accumulate over time by design. Reviews, mentions and links are meant to be slow, because that is what makes them hard to fake.
  • You are being ranked against incumbents. A competitor with four years of reviews has a head start that four weeks of work does not erase.

What moves in the first 90 days

The early wins are real but narrow. Expect movement here first:

  1. Long-tail and low-competition searches. "EICR certificate Havant" moves long before "electrician Portsmouth".
  2. Map pack visibility close to your base. Proximity works in your favour nearest to your address.
  3. Branded searches. People who already heard your name find you properly, which is worth more than it sounds.
  4. Profile engagement. Calls, direction requests and website clicks from the profile itself.

How to get there faster

  • Fix the profile first, properly. Categories, services, service area, hours, real photographs. It is the fastest-moving asset you have.
  • Make review requests automatic. A request that fires after a job is completed will always beat one that depends on remembering.
  • Narrow your target. Winning three towns properly beats appearing weakly in twelve.
  • Publish pages people actually need. Pricing guidance, what to expect, how quickly you attend. Useful beats optimised.
  • Answer the phone. The most common cause of "SEO isn't working" is enquiries arriving and not being picked up.

Does it differ by trade?

Yes, mostly through urgency and competition. Emergency trades such as electricians, plumbers and locksmiths have searches with immediate intent, so map pack gains convert quickly, but competition is fiercer. Planned work such as landscaping, extensions and kitchen fitting has longer research cycles, so the traffic converts more slowly but the jobs are larger and the terms are often less contested.

What AI search changes about the timeline

AI Overviews and assistants have not changed how long trust takes to build, but they have changed how quickly good information gets used. A page that answers a specific question clearly can be drawn into a generated answer without needing years of authority behind it, because the system is looking for the best passage rather than the strongest domain.

The practical effect for a trades business is that plainly written, specific pages, the ones covering what you charge, how fast you attend, which areas you cover, can earn visibility earlier than they would have in a purely link-driven ranking system. It does not shortcut the map pack, which still runs on profile quality and reviews.

How to tell it is working before the leads arrive

Leads are a lagging indicator. These move first, and if they are moving you are on track even if the phone has not changed yet:

  • Impressions rising in Google Search Console for service and town queries.
  • Profile searches shifting from branded to discovery in the performance report.
  • More queries appearing at all, even at low positions, meaning Google is testing you for more searches.
  • Average position improving on terms you were not visible for at the start.

Questions people ask

How long until I see the first result?

Usually 4 to 8 weeks for early signals such as profile views and impressions, assuming the Google Business Profile needed work. Businesses with an already-optimised profile move more slowly at the start because the easy gains are gone.

Why is my competitor ranking above me after years of my trading?

Trading history is not a ranking signal; profile quality, reviews, relevance and web presence are. A newer business with a well-built profile and a steady review flow will outrank an older one that never claimed its listing.

Can I speed it up by paying more?

Up to a point. More budget buys more pages, more outreach and faster execution, which compresses the timeline. It does not remove the waiting built into how Google accumulates trust, and anyone promising it does is describing something else.

What if nothing has changed after six months?

Check the leading indicators before concluding it failed. If impressions and profile views are flat as well as leads, something is wrong with the work or the targeting. If impressions are up but enquiries are not, the problem is usually the website or the phone, not the ranking.

Do results stop if I stop?

Not immediately, and that is the difference from paid ads. Rankings decay slowly as competitors keep working and reviews go stale, so you typically hold position for a while and then drift. Directory leads stop the day you stop paying.
