The Bournemouth market, specifically

Bournemouth mixes Victorian and Edwardian housing near the centre with large interwar and postwar suburbs further out, and it carries an unusually high share of rental stock, student lets and holiday flats. That rental density matters for trades: landlord certificates, end-of-tenancy work and quick turnarounds between lets make up a steady share of enquiries, and they cluster in Boscombe, Winton and Charminster rather than spreading evenly.

What competition looks like here

The BH postcodes are contested because Bournemouth, Poole and Christchurch sit almost continuously together, so a business ranking well in central Bournemouth is often competing with firms based in Poole that Google treats as equally close.
