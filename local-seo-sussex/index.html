The Sussex market, specifically

Sussex splits into two counties with quite different markets: a dense, competitive coastal strip and a more spread-out inland and rural belt. Coastal towns bring salt-air maintenance and a heavy rental and holiday-let sector; inland towns like Horsham and Crawley behave more like commuter markets with larger planned jobs.
